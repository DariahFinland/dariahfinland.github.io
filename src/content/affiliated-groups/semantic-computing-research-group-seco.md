The local node in the Aalto University is situated at the Department of Computer Science, Semantic Computing Research Group ([SeCo](https://seco.cs.aalto.fi/)), that has affiliated members also at the Helsinki Centre for Digital Humanities ([HELDIG](https://heldig.fi/)) and Helsinki Institute for Social Sciences and Humanities ([HSSH](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/helsinki-institute-social-sciences-and-humanities)). Our focus is on developing and maintaining a national Linked Open Data (LOD) infrastructure and applications for Digital Humanities. Specifically, we work on the [Sampo series](https://seco.cs.aalto.fi/applications/sampo/) of LOD services on the Linked Data Finland platform [LDF.fi](http://LDF.fi), tools for creating and publishing LOD, such as the [Sampo-UI framework](https://seco.cs.aalto.fi/tools/sampo-ui/), and on semantic portals with data-analytic tools for knowledge discovery. There are over 20 Sampo portals on the Web that have had up to millions of users.
