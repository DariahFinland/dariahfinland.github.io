The Helsinki Digital Humanities Summit is a scientific conference hosted yearly by the Helsinki Center for Digital Humanities (++[HELDIG](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/digital-humanities/community/heldig-helsinki-centre-digital-humanities)++) The HELDIG Summit brings together social sciences and humanities in a day that takes a snapshot at the state of the art in digital research. **This year, we bring discussion around methodological and infrastructure encounters beyond disciplines.** What challenges in data processing new methods enable us to overcome? How to maintain and adapt our research tools to fast-changing technological landscape? How to target the use of AI responsibly in the research cycle? 

**The Summit will take place at the University of Helsinki (Minerva Tori) on December 4 (we aim for a full-day 10:00-18:00)**, including a cocktail reception. The Summits are free and open to everyone, whatever their institution, discipline or career stage.

A **call for lightning talks and poster proposals is now open**. We are gathering contributions around three themes: digital history, digital social sciences and digital heritage and invite you to contribute to this event with a 5 minute flash-talk or poster from a recent or ongoing project. The Summit is a good opportunity to give visibility to latest research and tools, get inspired by methodological innovations in similar fields, and make new connections. 

To contribute with the programme fill ++[this form](https://elomake.helsinki.fi/lomakkeet/141465/lomake.html)++. The deadline for proposals in October 21st.
