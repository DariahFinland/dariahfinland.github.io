We are glad to introduce this month the recently created DARIAH-FI library

[https://www.zotero.org/groups/6332635/dariah-fi/library](https://www.zotero.org/groups/6332635/dariah-fi/library)

The point of departure of this library are cumulative publications aggregated since 2022, when DARIAH-FI started operations. In this virtual library one can find publications that explain a resource, data publications and guidelines to DARIAH-FI tools. This library is maintained by the network partners and will be continuously updated whenever [resources ](https://www.dariah.fi/resources-2/)are updated or related-research is published.

In this joint-library we point at:

- Publications related to DARIAH-FI activities such as surveys, community insights, etc
- Publications that point at and explain DARIAH-FI resources, including datasets and tools 
- Research that provide exemplary use cases of DARIAH-FI infrastructures
