The Helsinki Liberal Arts and Sciences Bachelor's is an interdisciplinary programme spanning six faculties. Relevant to DARIAH, it includes multiple specialisations combining data and computer science, mathematics and statistics, the social sciences, and the humanities, with attendant continuation options to the relevant master's programmes.
