DARIAH-Campus learning platform has just been updated with [new educational videos](https://campus.dariah.eu/resources)by digital humanities and social science researchers, projects and infrastructure developers in Finland.

This publication extends the outreach of an existing video library used as a teaching resource for BA Course [Introduction to Helsinki Digital Humanities and Social Science](http://dhcr.clarin-dariah.eu/courses/view/907) at the University of Helsinki.

**DARIAH-Campus** is a discovery framework and hosting platform for learning resources aimed at different profiles: students, teachers or researchers interested in incorporating digital humanities methods and digital scholarship in their practice. Today, it is one of the core services of the Pan-European Research Infrastructure for the Arts and Humanities ([DARIAH-EU](https://www.dariah.eu/)). Therefore, resources on DARIAH-Campus are often tied to this European infrastructure – but they are not limited to it. The project encourages contributions from scholars and teaching professionals from all around the digital humanities world.
