The Social Media Working Group organizes a brief introduction to CSC's services that might be helpful to those doing research on social media. Anni Järvenpää from CSC will introduce the services and answer questions from the audience. We will also hear a few words about the CSC ambassadors. Please register here: https://link.webropol.com/s/cscsome. If you have any questions, please contact us at paula.rautionaho@uef.fi.
