Weekly methodological discussions organised every Wednesday by the Helsinki Institute for Social Science and Humanities (HSSH). The idea of the meetings is to introduce methodological innovations and cutting-edge research in various disciplines in an easily accessible manner and have an interdisciplinary discussion in an easy-going atmosphere over lunch. The seminars are open to everybody. We expect a multidisciplinary and methodologically curious audience from different faculties and units of the central campus. The language of the meetings can be Finnish or English. Bring your own lunch, we bring fresh methodological topics!
