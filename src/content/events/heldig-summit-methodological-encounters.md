*This year we bring a line-up of some 50 research projects and infrastructures in the form of flash-talks and poster presentations in a day that takes a snapshot at **the state of the art in digital research through methodological encounters**. The event showcases the latest research, data, tools and methodologies at the intersection of historical, societal and cultural heritage perspectives. In addition to give a glipmse of digital SSH-resarch today, the summit wants to address following questions, what challenges in data-processing new methods enable us to overcome? How to maintain and adapt infrastructure to the fast-changing technological landscape? How to target the use of AI responsibly in the research cycle?* 

Join us on **December 4 from 10:30-18:00 at Minerva Plaza (Siltavuorenpenger 5A)** to get inspired, network across faculties and disciplines, or learn what is new in digital humanities and social science research. Check-out the full programme here: [https://www.helsinki.fi/en/helsinki-centre-for-digital-humanities/news/heldig-digital-humanities-summit-2026](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/helsinki-centre-for-digital-humanities/news/heldig-digital-humanities-summit-2026). 

We have limited seating capacity, so register for in-person attendance as soon as possilbe and no later than November 25 here [https://elomake.helsinki.fi/lomakkeet/142210/lomake.html](https://elomake.helsinki.fi/lomakkeet/142210/lomake.html) (a streaming link will be made available on the event page closer to the date, no need to register for that).

The HELDIG Summit is being organized by [Helsinki Centre for Digital Humanites (HELDIG)](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/digital-humanities/community/heldig-helsinki-centre-digital-humanities "https://www.helsinki.fi/en/digital-humanities/community/heldig-helsinki-centre-digital-humanities"), in collaboration with [Helsinki Centre for Intellectual History (HIC)](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/digital-humanities/community/heldig-helsinki-centre-digital-humanities "https://www.helsinki.fi/en/digital-humanities/community/heldig-helsinki-centre-digital-humanities"), [DIVSOL - Diversity in Society and Life](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/diversity-society-and-life/research "https://www.helsinki.fi/en/diversity-society-and-life/research"), [AI for SSH Research Network](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/diversity-society-and-life/news/ai-ssh-research-network-launched-kick-event "https://www.helsinki.fi/en/diversity-society-and-life/news/ai-ssh-research-network-launched-kick-event").
